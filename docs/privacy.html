Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Cobble Tales is a personal, non-commercial automation operated by a single individual for their own Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not a public-facing product and does not collect data from, or about, other people.

What data is used

The app uses its own operator's Instagram and TikTok API access tokens, obtained directly from the operator's own accounts, solely to publish videos the operator generated. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to viewers, followers, or any third party.

Data retention

Generated videos are held only long enough to be published to the operator's own accounts and are then deleted from temporary storage.

Third-party services

The app uses the Instagram Graph API and the TikTok Content Posting API strictly to publish content to the operator's own accounts, under each platform's terms of service.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account owner via the associated social media accounts.
