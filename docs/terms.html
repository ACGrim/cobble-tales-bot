Cobble Tales ("we", "the app") is a personal, non-commercial automation that posts short-form video content to social media on behalf of its own owner/operator. It is not offered as a service to the public and does not accept sign-ups from other users.

What the app does

The app generates short narration scripts using an AI language model, styled after viral "Reddit story" posts, and renders them as narrated videos over gameplay footage. It then automatically publishes those videos to the owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Content

All narration content is original text produced by an AI model for this purpose. The app does not repost or redistribute anyone else's copyrighted written work.
