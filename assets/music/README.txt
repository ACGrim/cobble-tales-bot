Put 2-4 royalty-free MP3 background music tracks in this folder before your
first real run. assemble_video.py picks one at random each day and mixes it
in quietly under the narration. If this folder is empty, videos are still
built fine — they just have no music bed.

Where to get tracks you're actually safe to monetize with:
  - YouTube's own Audio Library (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library) — the
    monetization guide explicitly recommends this, since it's pre-cleared
    for YouTube monetized content. Download as MP3 and drop the files here.
  - Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music) — free, no attribution required for
    most tracks, but double-check each track's license.

Do NOT pull random music off the internet or use commercial songs — that's
the fastest way to get the channel demonetized or copyright-struck.
